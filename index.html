What Basta does

You choose the apps, categories and websites that distract you. Basta groups them into focus modes (Work, Study, Sleep…) and blocks them on a weekly schedule, when you arrive at a place you choose, or for a quick session you start with one tap.

Common questions

Basta asks for Screen Time access. Why?

Blocking apps on iPhone is only possible through Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple shows the selected apps to Basta as anonymous tokens — we never learn which apps you picked, and your usage never leaves your phone.

A block didn't start on schedule

Check that Screen Time is on (Settings → Screen Time) and that Basta still has access there. Schedules shorter than 15 minutes are extended to 15 minutes by iOS.

Location-based modes don't trigger

Location modes need "Always" location access for Basta (Settings → Basta → Location). iOS can take a few minutes to notice that you arrived or left.

I turned on strict mode and can't stop the block

That is what strict mode is for: the block cannot be switched off until the session ends. It ends by itself at the end time you set.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge.

How do I delete my data?

Basta has no accounts and stores nothing about you on our side. Deleting the app removes everything it kept on the device, including its Screen Time restrictions.
