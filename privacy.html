Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Basta is published by Zintex OÜ, a company registered in Estonia ("we"). This policy explains what the Basta iPhone app does with your information.

The short version. Everything stays on your iPhone. Basta has no accounts, no servers that receive your data, and no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. We cannot see which apps you use, how long you use them, or where you are.

Screen Time data

Basta uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to block the apps you select. iOS gives Basta only opaque tokens for the apps, categories and websites you choose — not their names. Usage statistics shown in the app are rendered by an Apple-sandboxed extension; that data cannot be read by the app itself, is never sent anywhere, and is never seen by us.

Location

If you create a mode that turns on at a place, Basta asks for location access and uses iOS region monitoring to notice when you arrive at or leave that place. The places you save and the location events are processed on your device only. We never receive your location, and nothing about it is stored outside your phone. If you don't use location modes, Basta never asks for location.

What is stored on your device

  • Your focus modes: names, selected app tokens, schedules, saved places and settings.
  • Whether you completed onboarding.

This is kept in the app's private storage on your iPhone and removed when you delete the app.

What we do not collect

  • No account, email or password — the app has no sign-in.
  • No name, phone number, contacts or photos.
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising, analytics or attribution SDKs.
  • No list of the apps you use or block, and no usage history.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your card details. The app checks with Apple, on your device, whether your subscription is active. Apple may share anonymous sales reports with us that do not identify you.

Diagnostics

If you have Apple's "Share with App Developers" setting switched on, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. They contain technical information about the crash only. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Basta is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request. If you believe we hold something about you, write to info@zintex.dev. Under the GDPR you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and mention the change in the app's release notes.
